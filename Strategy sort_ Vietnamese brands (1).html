Strategy sort

Each group analyses one Vietnamese brand. Match every situation card to the right strategy level, STPD step and digital P of the marketing mix, write why, then present your board to the class.

Tap a card, then press Place here on the box where it belongs (or drag it there). Write one sentence explaining your choice. Reveal the answers only after your group has presented.

Situations are simplified for class discussion and based on publicly known information about each brand. Some cards could be argued two ways; a well-justified alternative answer is worth discussing.
